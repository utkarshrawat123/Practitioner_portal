# Dosing Principles

> **SAMPLE — replace with approved clinical content before live use.**

- Doses are always quoted verbatim from the product label dosing in each dossier —
  the assistant never invents, scales, combines, or "optimises" doses.
- Food-Grown® nutrients are taken with or after food unless the label states otherwise.
- Protocols should normally contain 1–4 products; fewer, well-rationalised products
  are preferred over long lists.
- Suggested review point for any protocol: 8–12 weeks, or sooner where symptoms change.
- Where two products contain overlapping nutrients (e.g. a multi plus a single mineral),
  the overlap must be pointed out in the rationale.
