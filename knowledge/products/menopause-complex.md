# Food-Grown® Menopause Complex

> **Clinical review:** AWAITING APPROVAL — not for live practitioner use.
> Drafted from public wildnutrition.com product information for pipeline testing only.

## Key ingredients
Food-Grown® B vitamins, magnesium and zinc with sage, red clover and ashwagandha,
formulated for the menopause transition.

## Label dosing
2 capsules daily, taken with or after food.

## Mechanism & evidence notes
Vitamin B6 contributes to the regulation of hormonal activity; B vitamins and
magnesium contribute to the reduction of tiredness and fatigue and to normal
psychological function (EFSA authorised claims). Sage has traditional use for
menopausal hot flushes. Practitioners commonly consider it as foundational
support through perimenopause and menopause alongside dietary and lifestyle work.

## Cautions & interactions
- Not for use in pregnancy or breastfeeding.
- Contains red clover (isoflavones) — hormone-sensitive conditions and tamoxifen require the client's medical team's involvement.
- Contains ashwagandha — caution with thyroid medication, sedatives and immunosuppressants; discontinue 2 weeks before surgery.
- Do not combine with other high-dose B-complex or magnesium products without reviewing total intakes (point out overlaps, e.g. with Magnesium (Food-Grown®)).
