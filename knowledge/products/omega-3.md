# Omega 3 (Pure Deep-Sea Fish Oil)

> **SAMPLE — replace with approved clinical content before live use.**

## Key ingredients
Pure deep-sea fish oil providing EPA and DHA per two-capsule dose, certified
sustainable, independently tested for oxidation and contaminants.

## Label dosing
2 capsules daily, taken with food.

## Mechanism & evidence notes
EPA and DHA contribute to the normal function of the heart (EFSA authorised claim,
≥250mg EPA+DHA daily); DHA contributes to maintenance of normal brain function and
vision. Practitioners commonly consider omega-3 status alongside dietary oily-fish
intake, particularly in vegetarian-leaning diets.

## Cautions & interactions
- Not suitable for people with fish allergy.
- Caution with anticoagulant/antiplatelet medication (warfarin, DOACs, aspirin) — high-dose fish oil may increase bleeding tendency; practitioner supervision required.
- Not a vegetarian/vegan product.
