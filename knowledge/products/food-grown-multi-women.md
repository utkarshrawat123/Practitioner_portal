## Key ingredients
Comprehensive Food-Grown® multivitamin and mineral formulated for women,
including B vitamins, vitamin D, iron, zinc, selenium and iodine in food-state form.

## Label dosing
3 capsules daily, taken with or after food (can be split across the day).

## Mechanism & evidence notes
Formulated as foundational micronutrient support for women through adulthood and
perimenopause. B6 contributes to the regulation of hormonal activity; iodine and
selenium contribute to normal thyroid function (EFSA authorised claims).

## Cautions & interactions
- Contains iron — see Iron Plus cautions for interaction spacing (levothyroxine, tetracyclines).
- Contains iodine — caution with thyroid conditions under medical management.
- Do not combine with other high-dose multivitamins without reviewing total intakes.
