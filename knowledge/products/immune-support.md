# Immune Support

> **Clinical review:** AWAITING APPROVAL — not for live practitioner use.
> Drafted from public wildnutrition.com product information for pipeline testing only.

## Key ingredients
Food-Grown® vitamin C, vitamin D, zinc and selenium with beta-glucans and
elderberry in a food-state matrix.

## Label dosing
2 capsules daily, taken with or after food.

## Mechanism & evidence notes
Vitamins C and D, zinc and selenium each contribute to the normal function of the
immune system (EFSA authorised claims). Practitioners commonly consider it as
seasonal foundational support or where dietary assessment suggests low intakes of
these micronutrients.

## Cautions & interactions
- Contains zinc — long-term intakes above the label dose can impair copper status; never exceed label dosing.
- Contains vitamin D — review total vitamin D intake across all supplements to avoid duplication.
- Caution with immunosuppressant medication — immune-active botanicals require the client's medical team's involvement.
- Separate zinc-containing products from tetracyclines and quinolones by 2–4 hours.
