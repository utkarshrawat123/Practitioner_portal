# High Fibre Plus

> **Clinical review:** AWAITING APPROVAL — not for live practitioner use.
> Drafted from public wildnutrition.com product information for pipeline testing only.

## Key ingredients
Blend of soluble and insoluble plant fibres (including psyllium and flaxseed)
with food-state B vitamins to support normal digestive function.

## Label dosing
2 capsules daily, taken with or after food and a full glass of water.

## Mechanism & evidence notes
Dietary fibre contributes to normal bowel function; psyllium husk in particular
has an established role in maintaining regularity and supporting normal blood
cholesterol levels at adequate intakes. Practitioners commonly consider it where
dietary fibre intake is assessed as low, alongside dietary change rather than in
place of it.

## Cautions & interactions
- Must be taken with adequate fluid — inadequate water intake with fibre supplements risks obstruction.
- Separate from oral medication by 1–2 hours — fibre can reduce absorption of some medicines.
- Caution in swallowing difficulties, oesophageal narrowing or existing GI obstruction (contraindicated).
- Introduce gradually where fibre intake has been low to minimise bloating.
