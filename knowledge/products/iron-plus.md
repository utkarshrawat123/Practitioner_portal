# Iron Plus (Food-Grown®)

> **Clinical review:** AWAITING APPROVAL — not for live practitioner use.
> Drafted from public wildnutrition.com product information for pipeline testing only.

## Key ingredients
Food-Grown® iron providing 20mg elemental iron per capsule, with vitamin C,
molybdenum and B vitamins in a food-state matrix.

## Label dosing
1 capsule daily, taken with or after food.

## Mechanism & evidence notes
Iron contributes to normal formation of red blood cells and haemoglobin, normal
oxygen transport, and reduction of tiredness and fatigue (EFSA authorised claims).
Food-state iron is commonly selected by practitioners for clients reporting
gastric discomfort with conventional iron salts. Relevant where low ferritin has
been identified by an appropriate healthcare professional.

## Cautions & interactions
- Iron supplementation should follow confirmed low iron status — unsupervised iron loading is inappropriate.
- Keep out of reach of children — iron overdose is dangerous in children.
- Reduces absorption of levothyroxine, tetracyclines and quinolones — separate by 2–4 hours.
- Caution in haemochromatosis and iron-storage conditions (contraindicated).
