# SRI-81™ Shatavari Plus

> **Clinical review:** AWAITING APPROVAL — not for live practitioner use.
> Drafted from public wildnutrition.com product information for pipeline testing only.

## Key ingredients
SRI-81™ shatavari (Asparagus racemosus) root extract with Food-Grown® supporting
nutrients.

## Label dosing
1 capsule daily, taken with or after food.

## Mechanism & evidence notes
Shatavari is a botanical traditionally used in Ayurvedic practice and studied for
support through the menstrual cycle and menopause transition. Human evidence is
limited and largely traditional-use based — position as supportive within a wider
protocol, and defer to practitioner judgement on hormonal presentations.

## Cautions & interactions
- Not recommended in pregnancy or breastfeeding without practitioner supervision.
- Caution with known allergy to asparagus species.
- Theoretical interaction with diuretics and lithium — practitioner review required where either is prescribed.
- Hormone-sensitive conditions: defer to the practitioner and the client's medical team.
