# Lion's Mane Plus

> **Clinical review:** AWAITING APPROVAL — not for live practitioner use.
> Drafted from public wildnutrition.com product information for pipeline testing only.

## Key ingredients
Lion's mane (Hericium erinaceus) fruiting-body extract with supporting B vitamins
in a food-state matrix.

## Label dosing
2 capsules daily, taken with or after food.

## Mechanism & evidence notes
Lion's mane is a culinary-medicinal mushroom studied for cognitive performance,
focus and mood support; erinacines and hericenones are the constituents most
discussed in the literature. Practitioners commonly consider it in protocols for
concentration and cognitive resilience. Evidence in humans is early-stage —
position it as supportive, not therapeutic.

## Cautions & interactions
- Not recommended in pregnancy or breastfeeding (insufficient safety data).
- Caution with known mushroom allergy — discontinue on any hypersensitivity reaction.
- Theoretical additive effect with anticoagulant/antiplatelet medication — practitioner review required.
- Discontinue 2 weeks before surgery.
