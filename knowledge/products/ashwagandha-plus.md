## Key ingredients
KSM-66® ashwagandha root extract 500mg per two-capsule dose with Food-Grown®
magnesium and B vitamins.

## Label dosing
2 capsules daily, taken with or after food.

## Mechanism & evidence notes
Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera) is an adaptogenic herb studied for perceived
stress and sleep quality; KSM-66® is the most-studied root-only extract with
multiple randomised controlled trials at 300–600mg/day. Practitioners commonly
consider it in protocols for stress resilience and sleep onset.

## Cautions & interactions
- Not recommended in pregnancy or breastfeeding.
- Caution with thyroid medication — ashwagandha may increase thyroid hormone levels.
- Caution with sedatives and immunosuppressants.
- Discontinue 2 weeks before surgery.
