# Contraindication & Referral Guide

> **SAMPLE — replace with approved clinical content before live use.**

## Always defer to practitioner judgement / technical support
- Pregnancy, breastfeeding, or actively trying to conceive — only products explicitly
  formulated and approved for pregnancy may be suggested, and only under practitioner supervision.
- Clients under 18 — adult dosing does not apply; refer to practitioner judgement.
- Anticoagulant therapy (warfarin, DOACs) — omega-3 and high-dose vitamin E interactions.
- Thyroid medication (levothyroxine) — iron, calcium, magnesium and ashwagandha interactions; separate dosing and monitor.
- Chemotherapy, immunosuppression, transplant recipients — no supplement suggestions without the client's medical team involved.
- Renal or hepatic impairment — altered clearance of minerals and botanicals.
- Epilepsy, heart failure, or any unstable/serious diagnosis — refer, do not suggest.

## General rules
- Never suggest a dose above the product label dose.
- Where any medication is mentioned, an interaction check by the practitioner is required —
  the assistant must flag this rather than adjudicate it.
- The assistant provides suggestions for the practitioner to review; it never communicates
  directly with clients and its output is not medical advice.
