This note summarises why Wild Nutrition uses a Food-Grown® (food-state) delivery for
iron rather than isolated iron salts such as ferrous sulphate.

Food-state minerals are grown into a food matrix (a mineral-enriched lactobacillus
culture), so the mineral is delivered complexed with proteins, lipids and other food
components rather than as a naked salt. Practitioners commonly select food-state iron
for clients who report gastric discomfort — nausea, constipation — with conventional
iron salts, as tolerability is a frequent reason clients abandon iron supplementation.

Iron contributes to the normal formation of red blood cells and haemoglobin, to normal
oxygen transport in the body, and to the reduction of tiredness and fatigue. These are
authorised EFSA claims and describe normal physiological function; they are not disease
claims.

Interaction points worth teaching: iron reduces the absorption of levothyroxine and of
certain antibiotics (tetracyclines, quinolones), so doses should be separated by several
hours. Iron is contraindicated in iron-storage conditions such as haemochromatosis, and
should never be given to children except under direct medical supervision.
